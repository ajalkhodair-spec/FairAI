# Ethical Scope

Fairness is the directly operationalized ethical dimension. The implementation
computes demographic parity, equal opportunity, equalized odds, and subgroup
accuracy gaps, then applies explicit versioned policy thresholds.

Integrity, transparency, and accountability are supported as governance
properties through content addressing, canonical digest binding, role checks,
signed decisions, lifecycle constraints, and audit events. They are not
measured as complete ethical outcomes.

Responsibility, honesty, respect, leadership, compassion, civic
responsibility, loyalty, respect for law, and environmental concern are not
mathematically operationalized. The system must not decide institutional ethics
requirements automatically, and the implementation is not a complete solution
to ethical AI.

The classification and nonclaims are listed in
`outputs/major_revision/governance/ethics_scope_matrix.csv`.
