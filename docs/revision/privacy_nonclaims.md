# Privacy Non-Claims

The current implementation does not claim:

- differential privacy;
- resistance to membership inference or model inversion;
- confidentiality of model parameters or updates;
- secure multiparty aggregation;
- encrypted IPFS artifacts;
- metadata, timing, sender, or CID unlinkability;
- anonymity of participants;
- deletion of data already published to IPFS peers or a blockchain;
- protection after a local host, verifier key, or administrator is compromised.

Raw-data locality is an architectural data-minimization property, not a formal
privacy theorem.
