# Public Dataset and Ethics Statement Review

Author review is required before manuscript resubmission.

## Data Availability

- Adult is publicly available from UCI under CC BY 4.0.
- COMPAS is downloaded from a pinned ProPublica source and is not redistributed
  by this repository.
- Dataset checksums and preparation code are public.
- Generated partitions and aggregate experiment evidence may be archived when
  they do not violate source terms or expose row-level records.

## Institutional Review

The implementation uses public secondary datasets and does not recruit
participants or collect new human-subject data. Whether institutional review,
exemption, or a formal determination is required depends on the authors'
institution and jurisdiction. The software cannot make that determination.

The manuscript's IRB, ethics, and data-availability statements must be reviewed
and approved by the responsible author and institution.
